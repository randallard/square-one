## Why that order

pnpm 12 writes `pnpm-lock.yaml` as two YAML documents, pnpm's own packages first. OSV-Scanner
v2.3.8, which `ci.yml` pinned, reads only the first, so after a pnpm 12 move the `osv-scan` job
would scan pnpm and nothing else and still say "No issues found" (template ADR-1018). So the
scanner moved first.

## What changed

- `c766cb2`: template ADRs 1018 and 1019 copied in, with OSV v2.7.0 in `ci.yml`. ADR-1019 (HTTP APIs
  linted against a vendored api-standards ruleset) is n/a here: its `api lint` job self-skips,
  because a library has no OpenAPI document. The template's skills and reviews README are in again,
  and this repo's SHA-pin wording on the OSV call is kept. The 11-01 compliance note and stance

- The next commit: `packageManager` is `pnpm@12.16.1`, and the lockfile gains a second document
  of 158 lines. Nothing else in it moved. Frozen install, lint, 272 tests, build, `pnpm audit`
  (1 ignored, unchanged) and `audit signatures` (316) all pass.
